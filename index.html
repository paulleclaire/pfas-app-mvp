PFAS Calculator

PFAS in drinking water should be interpreted alongside total PFAS intake from all sources. EFSA’s Tolerable Weekly Intake (TWI) for the sum of PFOS, PFOA, PFNA and PFHxS is 4.4 ng/kg/week from all sources. The UK 100 ng/L figure is a Tier 3 action level for the sum of 20 PFAS, not a health-based safe limit. Water Awareness Jersey calls for testing, filtration and transparent public reporting.

This Version 1 calculator is based on the four PFAS used in the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) Tolerable Weekly Intake.

ng/L (ppt)

Enter the combined concentration of these four PFAS:

  • PFOS
  • PFOA
  • PFHxS
  • PFNA

If your laboratory reports them separately, add the four values together. If your water supplier already reports the sum of these four PFAS, simply enter that number. Do not enter a 'Total PFAS' value or a different PFAS group unless it specifically states it is the sum of PFOS + PFOA + PFHxS + PFNA.

For drinking water, 1 ng/L is exactly the same as 1 part per trillion (1 ppt), so no conversion is required.

Examples
  • Jersey Water average (2025): 12 ng/L (12 ppt)
  • Jersey Independent Scientific Advisory Panel recommendation: 4 ng/L (4 ppt)

How this calculation works

All figures are the sum of PFOS, PFOA, PFNA and PFHxS. Recommended water intake is a hydration reference (30 mL per kg for adults, 35 mL per kg for children), not a limit.

Version 1 estimates drinking-water contribution using typical intake assumptions for adults and children. It does not model pregnancy, fetal exposure, breastfeeding, organ development, long-term PFAS accumulation or individual body burden. Those are planned for a future PFAS Exposure Explorer Version 2.

About this App

This app shows that when PFAS is present in your drinking water, the dose you get simply by drinking enough to stay alive, can exceed your immune-safe limit — even before eating any food. Based upon the logic of a weekly dietary maximum tolerable intake, before you exceed the safe weekly limit, set out by the European Food Safety Authority. It uses the EFSA scientific safety standard: 4.4 ng per kg body weight per week. No spin. Just maths. PFAS are industrial chemicals that accumulate in your blood for years. Once inside you, they stay. EFSA examined animal studies, human biomonitoring, and vaccine-response data in children. Their scientists found one critical effect: children exposed to higher PFAS had weaker antibody responses to vaccines. They traced this back using PBPK modelling and set a strict weekly limit — the Tolerable Weekly Intake (TWI). This limit is the scientific safe-dose. • EFSA TWI: 4.4 nanograms per kilogram of body weight per week • Based on immune suppression in children Everything in this app is built on that number. What this calculator does: 1. Uses typical daily water intake for your age group 2. Calculates your weekly PFAS “budget” using EFSA’s formula 3. Uses your water’s PFAS level 4. Shows how much water you can drink before you exceed that dose The logic is simple: • If you exceed the EFSA limit, your immune system is under strain • If you do that every week, PFAS builds up in your blood • If you do that for years, you’re at chronic risk This app exposes that risk with clarity. No distractions. No spin. Just the numbers. Let them speak.
